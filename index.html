Baseline architecture

Primary goals: upper-body hypertrophy; +30 kg × 5 weighted pull-up within 6 months; ski conditioning in 4 months. Three lifting days remain the starting architecture, but the AI coach is free to modify the programme when evidence and your data support doing so.

Coaching philosophy

Optimise aggressively for sustainable hypertrophy and strength gain. Progress when performance, RPE, technique, recovery and symptoms support it. Do not hold back load or volume merely from excessive caution, but do not trade long-term progress for deteriorating technique, persistent joint/tendon symptoms or clearly unrecovered performance.

Nutrition baseline: maintenance to small surplus; protein ~1.6–2.2 g/kg/day (about 120–165 g/day at 75 kg). Avoid a conventional aggressive bulk because added body mass directly increases the system load of a pull-up.

AI coaching exchange

The HTML does not interpret performance. Export sends raw workouts, every set/RPE, notes, programme context and bodyweight to the AI. The AI returns structured JSON containing reviews and future prescriptions.
